About this map

Puerto Rico Reservoir Watersheds

An interactive view of six reservoirs in the mountains of Puerto Rico, the watersheds that drain to them, and the public data that describes them.

What it shows

Reservoirs and drainage areas, rivers, stream and rain gauges, hurricane tracks, terrain in 2D and 3D, landslides, land cover, hydrologic soil groups, soil erodibility, geology, faults, roads, and the tunnels, canals, and dams that move water between basins. Every layer comes from a public federal source listed in the data catalog.

Reservoir figures

Storage capacities and sedimentation figures on the reservoir cards come from published USGS sedimentation survey reports. Each card links to its report.

Watershed boundaries

Study watersheds are the HUC12 subwatersheds mapped at each dam in the USGS Watershed Boundary Dataset. Their published areas are the drainage areas shown on the reservoir cards.

Cross sections

Profiles sample the 3DEP terrain model, about 10 meter resolution inside the study watersheds and about 30 meters elsewhere. Elevations are in feet.

This is a data viewer, not a design document. Confirm any measurement against the original sources before using it in an engineering decision.
